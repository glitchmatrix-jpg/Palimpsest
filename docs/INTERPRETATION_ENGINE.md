# Interpretation Engine — Design Notes

Every multi-card reading must produce both:

1. position-aware individual interpretations
2. a genuinely computed holistic interpretation

## Analysis layers

### Card layer
- card identity
- orientation
- spread position
- themes
- element
- number / court role
- Major vs Minor

### Pair layer
Adjacent and structurally relevant cards are compared for:
- reinforcement
- contrast
- progression
- release
- tension
- repetition

### Spread layer
Aggregate:
- dominant themes
- suit / element emphasis
- Major Arcana concentration
- repeated numbers
- court patterns
- reversal concentration
- emotional / narrative trajectory

### Narrative synthesis
Convert structural signals into:
- opening state
- movement / tension
- likely direction
- explanation of why the cards connect

Internal weights are never presented as fortune probabilities.
